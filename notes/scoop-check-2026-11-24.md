# Scoop check, 2026-11-24 (before starting)

Question: has anyone reported whether the Gaia-predicted photometric microlensing event of G 207-14
(late July 2025), or any other photometric event predicted from Gaia, actually happened?

Searches (web search + OpenAlex, 2026-11-24):
1. "predicted photometric microlensing events Gaia DR3 2025 2026": only prediction papers (Bramich 2018;
   Bramich & Nielsen 2018; Mustill et al. 2018; McGill et al. 2019, 2023; Kluter et al. 2018, 2022).
2. "first detection of a predicted photometric microlensing event ...": none. The only followed-up
   predictions are astrometric (Stein 2051B with HST, Sahu et al. 2017; Proxima Cen with VLT, Zurlo et
   al. 2018; LAWD 37 with HST, McGill et al. 2023, arXiv:2206.01814).
3. The Gaia IDs "2042703905028908928" / "2030898139472914688" / "4288183244850920192" + microlensing:
   only Mustill et al. 2018.
4. "G 207-14" microlensing 2025; "2042703900727995008" OR "LSPM J1905+3110": only Mustill et al. 2018.
5. OpenAlex: all 16 works citing Mustill et al. 2018 (W4289632714). None reports an observation of the
   2025 events. The closest is McGill et al. 2020 (MNRAS 498, L6; arXiv:2006.13958), "Predictions of
   Gaia's prize microlensing events are flawed": spurious DR2 sources and quality cuts, with no event
   observations.
6. "arXiv 2026 predicted microlensing event nearby M dwarf detected ZTF ...": nothing relevant. The new
   arXiv:2611.24561 (Gaia alert events whose masses DR4 can verify) is a different question.
7. ALeRCE ZTF alert broker, cone of 10" on G 207-14: two single-detection, unclassified alerts
   (ZTF25aafoitk at MJD 60721, ZTF25aathhkk at MJD 60826), both before the window. No reported
   microlensing event or TNS/ATel entry was found.

Closest prior work:
- Mustill, Davies & Lindegren 2018 (arXiv:1805.11638) made the prediction.
- Kluter et al. 2022 (arXiv:2112.12152) made the eDR3 re-prediction and catalogue.
- McGill et al. 2020 (arXiv:2006.13958) is the critique of prediction reliability.
- McGill et al. 2023 is the first followed-up *astrometric* prediction.

What would be new: the first test of a Gaia-predicted *photometric* microlensing event against
survey data, and the first systematic scorecard of such predictions within ZTF's era.
